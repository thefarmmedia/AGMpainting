Our story

Built on integrity, one room at a time.

AGM Painting LLC is owned and operated by Anthony Runyon in Springfield, Missouri. What started as one painter who cared more than most has grown into a trusted local company serving homeowners and businesses across the Springfield area.

We’re a small, family-focused business, and we like it that way. It means Anthony is hands-on with every project. The person who gives you the estimate is invested in how your walls look when we leave. We specialize in high-end painting, where clean lines, smooth finishes and careful prep separate a good job from a great one.

Our favorite part of the job is the look of satisfaction on a customer’s face when the project is finished. We take pride in our work, and our customers notice. They talk about our punctuality, our professionalism and taking the time to explain the process and help pick the right colors.

What you can count on

  • Honest, clear estimates with fair pricing
  • We show up on time and communicate
  • Prep done right: patch, caulk, scrape, prime
  • Your home and landscaping protected
  • Help choosing colors and sheens
  • A clean job site and a final walkthrough
