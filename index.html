Frequently asked questions

What areas does AGM Painting serve?

We’re based in Springfield, MO and serve about a 30-mile radius, including Nixa, Ozark, Republic, Battlefield, Willard, Strafford, Rogersville, Fair Grove, Clever, Billings, Sparta, Marshfield, Ash Grove, Highlandville, Fremont Hills, Brookline and Walnut Grove.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Fill out the form at the top of this page or call (417) 224-0103 and we’ll set up a time to look at your project and give you a clear, written estimate.

What kinds of painting do you do?

Interior and exterior residential painting, commercial painting, exterior wood and cedar siding staining, black exposed basement ceilings, doors, trim and garage doors, and the drywall repair and prep that comes before paint.

How do you protect my home while you work?

Prep is half the job. We cover floors with paper and plastic, mask windows, trim and fixtures, move and cover furniture, and protect landscaping outside. Then we clean up completely when the job is done.

Who will be painting my home?

AGM Painting is a small, family-focused business run by owner Anthony Runyon. Anthony is hands-on with every project, so the person who gives your estimate is invested in how the job turns out.
