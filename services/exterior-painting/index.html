Exterior Painting done right, from prep to final coat

Southwest Missouri weather is tough on paint. Hot, humid summers, hard UV and freeze-thaw winters make bad prep show up as peeling within a year or two. That’s why every AGM exterior job starts with washing, scraping loose paint, sanding edges smooth, priming bare wood and caulking gaps.

Whether you’re refreshing a ranch home in Springfield, updating dated siding before you sell or saving a peeling garage or barn-style shed, we use quality exterior coatings and take care around your landscaping. The result is a crisp, durable finish that boosts curb appeal.

Good questions

Exterior Painting FAQs

What’s the best time of year to paint exterior in Springfield?

Late spring through mid-fall is ideal, roughly April to October, when temperatures stay above 50°F and humidity is reasonable. We watch the forecast closely and schedule around rain.

Why is my exterior paint peeling?

Usually it’s moisture or poor prep: paint over chalky, dirty or failing coats, or bare wood that was never primed. We remove loose paint, prime bare spots and seal gaps so the new coat bonds properly.

Do you paint sheds and detached garages?

Absolutely. Barn-style sheds, detached garages and outbuildings are some of our favorite before-and-afters. A scraped, primed and repainted shed looks new again.
