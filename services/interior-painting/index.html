Interior Painting done right, from prep to final coat

Fresh interior paint is the fastest way to make a home feel new again, provided the prep is right. AGM Painting covers floors, masks every edge and fixes nail pops and dings before the first coat goes on. That’s why our cut lines stay crisp and our finishes stay even.

From a single bedroom to a whole-house repaint, we help you choose colors and sheens that suit how each room gets used. Then we leave the place cleaner than we found it. Good questions

Interior Painting FAQs

How long does it take to paint a room?

Most standard bedrooms take one day, prep included. Larger rooms, high or vaulted ceilings, dark-to-light color changes and wood paneling can take two. We’ll give you a timeline with your estimate.

Do I need to move my furniture?

Clear out small items, décor and anything fragile. We’ll move larger furniture to the center of the room, cover it and protect your floors with drop cloths, paper or plastic.

Can you paint over wood paneling?

Yes. Paneling needs the right primer to bond and to stop tannins bleeding through. We clean, scuff, prime and finish it so it looks like a smooth, modern wall.
